Play-by-play

Good evening, and welcome back to the losers bracket of the 2026 1v1 Bracket, where every set is an elimination set. Four players remain. After tonight, there will be three.

Modus arrives red hot. He took CoreDawg apart 3–0 in thirteen minutes of total game time, and he did it on Toxin and on Snowy Drought, his map and his faction.

Waiting for him is Tytan, the number two seed. He swept Pancake, got swept by WildCard, then outlasted OneThree111 3–1 in a set that included a 21-minute marathon on Egyptian Oasis.

Here's the part that makes this one special. On the same team, Modus and Tytan are 53–8, and each player's profile lists the other as his top teammate. Put them on opposite teams and the record is 79–79. That's 158 games, split exactly down the middle. Tonight one of them goes home.

2f · If it goes the distanceGame seven is a mirror

In a best of 7, maps come in swapped pairs for games one to six, which leaves game seven as the only unpaired game. Every series in this bracket that reached its final game settled it with both players on the same general. Pancake and Skip played an Infantry mirror. Gorn beat Modus in an Infantry mirror. Gorn beat WildCard in a Demolition mirror. If that tradition holds, the last pick is "which general do we both play?"

Segment 6

Keys to victory

What Modus has to do

Drag it into the desert

  1. Put plain GLA in every pairHe's 15–3 on GLA generals in 1v1s and 16–1 against them, and Tytan is 4–5 against them. Plain GLA is where the gap is widest: 66.0% for Modus, 54.5% for Tytan, which is also Tytan's least-played general. Pair GLA with anything except Air Force, Infantry or Demolition and the cell comes out blue. Toxin and Stealth are closer to even because Tytan is good on them too.
  2. Ask for a GLA mirror in game sevenThe comfort index has a plain GLA mirror at 59% Modus, the best single pick on the board. Last time he reached a decider he played an Infantry mirror against Gorn and lost. Infantry is Tytan's 68% general.
  3. Keep Tytan off Air Force and InfantryTytan is 4–0 on Air Force in 1v1s and wins 72.7% on it overall. Modus is 1–2 against Air Force and 0–3 against Infantry. Any pair with either of those is uphill.
  4. Go back to Snowy DroughtHe's 4–1 there in this bracket and has gone to it in all three series. Tytan is 0–2 there, both losses to WildCard.
  5. Skip ChinaThe model rates China as Modus's worst general (43% average). Stealth is his best by both the model (53%) and his career (69.1%).
What Tytan has to do

Take to the air

  1. Build pairs from Air Force, Infantry and DemolitionAir Force with Infantry is tied for the reddest cell on the board, about 40% for Modus. Those three are Tytan's top career generals at 72.7%, 68.0% and 70.4%, and also among the community's best 1v1 generals.
  2. Keep plain GLA out of the pairsTytan went 0–2 on plain GLA and Toxin against WildCard. A GLA pair runs up to 59% Modus. If Modus forces GLA, answer with Air Force, Infantry or Demolition; those are the only partners that pull a GLA pair back to even. Demolition is also Tytan's 4–0 1v1 general.
  3. Take him to Canyon of the DeadTytan is 3–0 there, with wins in 4:12, 3:24 and 5:36. Modus hasn't played it in the bracket.
  4. Strike first, end earlyHe's drawn first blood in 7 of 11 tournament games and is 4–1 in games under ten minutes. He's 2–4 in longer games, so the opening push has to do real damage.
  5. Make game seven an Infantry mirrorInfantry is the community's best 1v1 general, it's Tytan's 68% general, and it's the mirror that knocked Modus out of the winners bracket.

Color commentary · The booth's call

The model calls this a coin flip, with a hair's lean to Tytan. The swap format means neither player can steal it with one clever pick. Both players win fast, both lose long, and they've split 158 games as opponents.

The tiebreaker is GLA. Tytan has a losing record against it, Modus has nearly perfect numbers with it, and Modus has been on Toxin and Snowy Drought this week. If Tytan can keep the generals in the air and the games short, all of that goes out the window.
