  2. Survive minute twoCoreDawg's first kill usually lands around the two-minute mark. Losing a unit early is fine as long as Modus doesn't lose a supply truck or a dozer.
  3. Make it Snowy DroughtModus is 2–1 there this tournament and CoreDawg is 0–2. Their only career 1v1 was on Snowy Drought, and Modus won it in 4 minutes 27 seconds.
  4. End it before tenModus is 5–2 in games under ten minutes and 1–2 in longer ones. When he's ahead he should push and end the game.

  1. Turn first blood into economy damageHe drew first blood in three of four games against WildCard and still lost that series 1–3. Killing a unit isn't enough. He has to kill trucks and supply lines so Modus falls behind on money.

  3. Make it longHe's 4–2 in games past ten minutes. He has the income to back it up: 126,500 collected in a 14-minute win over Syn. The longer the game, the more it suits him.
  4. Bring the RaptorsAir Force is 2–0 for him in this tournament, including his only game win against WildCard. His USA-family core of Humvees and Missile Defenders shows up on four different generals.
  5. Ride the momentumHe has won six of his last seven games and played two weeks ago. Modus hasn't played a tournament game since August 21. Pressure him early, before he settles in.
