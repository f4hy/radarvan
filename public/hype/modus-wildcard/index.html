From the booth

Play-by-play

Good evening, and welcome to the Losers Final of the 2026 1v1 Bracket. Three players remain. Gorn is unbeaten and waiting in the Grand Final. Tonight decides who gets to go after Gorn.

Modus took the long way here. After losing the Winners Semifinal to Gorn in a game-seven Infantry mirror, Modus swept CoreDawg 3–0 and then beat Tytan 4–2 on October 23. Modus lost the opener on Snowy Drought, then won four of the last five, three of them after falling behind on the win-probability chart.

WildCard took the short way. WildCard beat CoreDawg 3–1, swept Tytan 4–0 in the Winners Semifinal, and then went the distance with Gorn in the Winners Final. Each of the first three map pairs split 1–1, and game seven was a Demolition mirror on TD NoBugsCars. It ran 21:54, the longest game of the bracket, and Gorn won it.

Here's the number hanging over tonight. In 1v1s, Modus is 6–0 against WildCard. That's the only opponent WildCard has never beaten one on one. But that record is old. Five of those games were played on a single night, April 24, six months ago. The most recent was September 5, seven weeks ago, two days before WildCard swept Tytan. WildCard needs four wins tonight.

The faction war

Modus has played nine different generals in this bracket. WildCard's best generals are on the USA side: Laser and plain USA. Here's how those two pools collide.

Across 298 community 1v1 games in the RadarVan database, the three factions form a loop. China beats USA. USA edges GLA. GLA edges China. Each arrow points from the winner to the loser.

At the general level, the community's best 1v1 generals are Infantry (63%), Demolition (55%) and USA (54%). The worst are Laser (41%) and Nuke (42%). That makes WildCard's Laser the most interesting pick in the series: the community loses with it, and it's WildCard's best career general at 57.6%.

2f · Sweep a pairSplits are the default

The bracket's best-of-7s have produced 12 swapped map pairs so far, and 9 of them split 1–1. Whoever wins both sides of a map usually wins the series. WildCard owns two of the three sweeps, both against Tytan on Vendetta and Snowy Drought. Modus owns the third, against Tytan on Canyon of the Dead.

2g · If it goes the distanceGame seven is a mirror

In a best of 7, maps come in swapped pairs for games one to six, which leaves game seven as the only unpaired game. Every series in this bracket that reached its final game settled it with both players on the same general, and both of tonight's players have lost one to Gorn. Modus lost an Infantry mirror on Oxygen 1. WildCard lost the 21:54 Demolition mirror.

Segment 6

Keys to victory

What Modus has to do

Make WildCard play GLA

  1. Put Toxin or Stealth in the pairThe swap means WildCard has to play whatever Modus picks. Toxin is WildCard's worst career general (43.7%) and Modus wins 66.7% on it. Stealth is Modus's best (69.1%). Toxin with Stealth is the greenest non-mirror pair on the board at 68%, and the Toxin mirror tops the mirror list at 70%.
  2. Keep it under ten minutesModus is 9–2 in bracket games under ten minutes and 4–4 in longer ones. WildCard is 5–2 past ten. One of the wins over Tytan took 5:12, on Infantry on Vendetta.
  3. Keep WildCard off Infantry and Air ForceIn 1v1s, Modus is 0–4 against Infantry and 1–3 against Air Force. Both of Modus's losses to Tytan came against those two generals. WildCard is 3–1 on Infantry and 2–0 on Air Force.
  4. Stay away from Laser and USAThey're the only blue cells on the board. Modus is 0–2 on Laser in 1v1s and wins only 50% on USA overall, which is Modus's lowest number.
  5. Trust the 6–0, carefullySix 1v1s, six wins, on five different generals, and two of them on Snowy Drought. But five came on April 24, six months ago, and the last one was September 5. They haven't met one on one since.
What WildCard has to do

Laser focus

  1. Build pairs from Laser and USALaser is WildCard's best career general (57.6%, 6–4 in 1v1s) and plain USA is 8–2 in 1v1s. Laser with USA is the bluest non-mirror pair on the board, about 52% WildCard, and the Laser mirror is WildCard's best game-seven option.
  2. Reach for Infantry and Air ForceModus is 1–7 against those two generals in 1v1s, and both of Modus's losses to Tytan came against them. Infantry is also the community's best 1v1 general at 63%.
  3. Drag it past ten minutesWildCard is 5–2 in long bracket games, and WildCard's career wins average 16.6 minutes. Modus is 4–4 once a game goes long.
  4. Take it to VendettaWildCard is 4–2 on the bracket's most-played map, including the 2–0 sweep of Tytan. Modus is 2–2 there.
  5. Sweep one pairMost map pairs split. WildCard swept two of them against Tytan. Do that once and WildCard needs only a split on the other two maps to win the series.
