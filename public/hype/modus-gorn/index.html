From the booth

Play-by-play

Good evening, and welcome to the Grand Final of the 2026 1v1 Bracket. Ten players started. Two are left, and they've met before.

On August 21, Gorn and Modus played the Winners Semifinal. Every map pair split, and Gorn won game seven, an Infantry mirror on Oxygen 1, by out-earning Modus 103,700 to 63,300. Modus dropped to the losers bracket and hasn't stopped since: 3–0 over CoreDawg, 4–2 over Tytan, and 4–1 over WildCard on November 1, including four straight wins on Canyon of the Dead.

Gorn went the other way: 4–3 over WildCard on September 26, after trailing that game seven with a 17% chance to win, and then a long wait. Gorn hasn't played a bracket game since, and went 1–5 in 1v1s on October 16.

The format makes it a different fight for each of them. Gorn needs one best of 9. Modus needs two: win this one to force a bracket reset, then win that one too.

Segment 8

Keys to victory

What Modus has to do

Take it to Gorn

  1. Fight on Gorn's side of the mapModus is 14–0 in bracket games where most of the fighting happens in the opponent's half and 3–7 otherwise. In August, the three Modus wins were all fought in Gorn's half (82% to 97% of the damage).
  2. Kill the economy earlyModus is 15–0 when destroying more economy than it loses. Gorn's game is built on earning more, with extra Supply Centers and supply trucks and every Oil Derrick in reach. The trucks are the target.
  3. Finish before ten minutesModus is 12–2 in bracket games under ten minutes; Gorn is 6–1 past ten. Every Modus win over Gorn in August took 8:40 or less.
  4. Don't raid into a wall on DemolitionModus is 0–3 on Demolition in this bracket. Two of those losses started with early raids into Missile Defenders that cost more than they killed, and in the third Gorn's rush kept Modus at home. When Demolition comes up, that's the danger game.
  5. Bank the leadsGorn has won three games from 17% or worse. A Modus lead isn't safe until Gorn's income is gone.
What Gorn has to do

Out-earn and outlast

  1. Bring the fight to Modus's baseGorn is 10–1 when most of the fighting is in the opponent's half. All four wins over Modus came that way, including the game-1 siege that never let Modus leave home.
  2. Build the economy, then use itGorn out-earned the opponent in 11 bracket games and won 10. The extra Supply Centers in game 7 paid for an army Modus couldn't match. But don't sit on it: Gorn averages 4,005 unspent.
  3. Drag it past ten minutes6–1 in long bracket games, and Modus is 5–5. Survive the early raids and the late game is Gorn's.
  4. Make the Infantry and Tank draws countGorn is 7–2 on Infantry in 1v1s, and Modus is 0–4 against it. Tank and USA are the closest pairs on the board. Those games are Gorn's best chances.
  5. Win onceThat's all it takes. Win this best of 9 and there's no reset.

Color commentary · The booth's call

On paper it's Modus. The model gives Modus 64% a game, which works out to about 80% for the Grand Final and roughly two in three to win both series. Modus has the better number on ten of twelve generals and has won 11 of 14 since the drop.

But Gorn is the only player in this bracket who has beaten Modus in a series, and Gorn did it the way Gorn wins everything: more Supply Centers, more money, and no panic at 15%. If Gorn's economy gets going and the games run past ten minutes, the model's number won't mean much.
