From the booth

Play-by-play

Good evening, and welcome back. We said it would take two series for Modus, and here we are at the second one.

On November 8, Modus beat Gorn 5–3 in the Grand Final and forced the bracket reset. Modus took the first two on Snowy Drought, including a game 2 where the model had Modus at 29% with a minute to play. Gorn took both on Natural Threats, winning from 11% and 15%. Arctic Lagoon split with the USA side winning both, and then TD NoBugsCars went to Modus twice, the last of them on Demolition, the general that had beaten Modus three times already in this bracket.

So the series is 1–1. Across two nights and fifteen games, Modus leads 8–7. Neither player has any margin left: the winner of this best of 9 is the 2026 champion.

Finish it early

  2. Kill the army as well as the economyGame 4 is the warning: Modus took every Supply Center and truck Gorn had by 7:07, and lost to the Tank Hunters already on the field.

  5. Have a plan for Natural Threats1–3 on the map. If it comes up, the Tank Hunter trades need to change.
What Gorn has to do

Make it long

  1. Bring the fight to Modus's baseGorn is 13–2 when most of the fighting is in the opponent's half and 1–10 otherwise. Don't wait for Modus to get there first.
  2. Spend the moneyGorn has out-earned the opponent in 14 bracket games and won 12. Game 2 is the exception worth remembering: 9,000 ahead, then 16 Red Guards into Modus's Humvees.
