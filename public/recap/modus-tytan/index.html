Analyst desk

Modus out-clicked Tytan in all six games, and the series still went six. The games Modus lost weren't lost on hands. In game 1, Modus spent early money on raids into a Missile Defender wall and then had no answer for Humvees in the worker line. In game 3, Modus won the big fights and let a few Tank Hunters and a Carpet Bomb take the supply line.

WildCard is next, and WildCard's best general is Laser. In this bracket WildCard's Laser games were built on Humvees and Missile Defenders (49 Humvees and 67 Missile Defenders against Tytan alone), which is the army that beat Modus in game 1. The pair Modus swept on Canyon of the Dead is the template: heavy units at home, Tank Hunters or MiGs to clean up, and steady pressure on the economy, without walking the army into the enemy's defense.
