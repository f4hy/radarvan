Analyst desk

Each of Modus's four wins came from getting somewhere first: into the worker line in games 1 and 4, and into WildCard's base before the army was built in games 2 and 5. The loss came from the habit the Tytan recap flagged: Demolition raiding into Missile Defenders. Modus did it in game 1 against Tytan and again in game 3 here, and lost both games.

Gorn is next. Gorn is unbeaten in this bracket and has already beaten Modus, 4–3 in the Winners Semifinal on August 21. Those seven games are the pair's entire 1v1 history, so Modus is 3–4 against Gorn. Coming from the losers bracket, Modus has to win two series: the Grand Final, to force a reset, and then the reset itself.

On this bracket's three maps, Modus's best is Canyon of the Dead (4–0) and Gorn's is Vendetta (4–2).
