Income decided this bracket more than anything else did. First blood was close to a coin flip, and so was being the faster player. The champion played at 47 actions a minute and the runner-up at 62.

Generals are assigned at random in every game, so nobody chose what they played. Across the field, GLA went 14–6 and Infantry 13–8. China went 2–9 and Toxin 5–10. Vendetta and Snowy Drought were the most-played maps, with 17 games each.
