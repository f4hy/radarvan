How close it was

Analyst desk

In six of the nine games, most of the fighting happened in the loser's half of the map. The exceptions were games 2 and 4, which Gorn won at home behind Fire Bases and Avengers, and game 6, which Modus won the same way with Humvees. Before this series Modus was 18–0 in bracket games fought mostly on the opponent's side. Games 2 and 4 were the first two losses.

Across the two finals Modus won nine games and Gorn won eight. Across the whole bracket they played 24 games and won 12 each. Gorn took two of the three series, both by a single game, and both in a decider.

Gorn is the 2026 1v1 Bracket champion: 19–16 in games, four series won, and 3–0 in deciding games. Modus finishes second at 26–15, the most game wins of anyone in the field.
