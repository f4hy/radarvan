Analyst desk

In seven of the eight games, the winner did most of the fighting on the other player's side of the map. The exception was game 2, which Modus won by letting Gorn's attack come to the Humvees. Modus is now 18–0 in bracket games where most of the fighting happens in the opponent's half.

The reset is one more best of 9, scheduled for November 14, and the winner takes the title. The series is 1–1, and in games Modus leads 8–7. Gorn's clearest path is still the long game: Gorn is 8–2 in bracket games of ten minutes or more, and Modus is 16–3 in the ones under ten.

On the maps that have been played by both, Modus's best is TD NoBugsCars (4–0) and Gorn's is Natural Threats (3–1).
